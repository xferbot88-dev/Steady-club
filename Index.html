Steady

Managing two conditions is one habit, practiced daily.

Steady is a members' club for people living with hypertension, diabetes, or both — plain-language lessons, short lectures, and a place to compare notes with people doing the same daily work.

01 lessons Short, plain-language guides on blood pressure, blood sugar, food, and movement — written to be read in one sitting.
02 lectures Video and audio sessions you can watch or listen to at your own pace, including relaxation and grocery-store walkthroughs.
03 chat A members-only board to ask questions and trade what's working — open once you've joined.
